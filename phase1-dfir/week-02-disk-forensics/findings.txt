- WHAT I LEARNED:
  I learned that digital forensics mandates absolute data preservation. Investigators 
  never work on live files; they compute a cryptographic hash of a disk image immediately 
  upon ingestion. If a single bit of data changes during analysis, the hashes will mismatch, 
  rendering the evidence completely inadmissible in a court of law or internal corporate audit.

- WHAT IT MEANS IN REAL LIFE:
  When an organisation experiences an insider threat incident or an active breach, 
  incident responders pull a raw physical copy of the hard drive. By capturing the cryptographic 
  fingerprint (hash value) and analyzing it inside a protected sandbox like Autopsy, they can 
  uncover deleted attacker timelines, exfiltrated files, and wiped browser histories while fully 
  protecting the chain of custody.
